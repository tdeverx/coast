# Checking for evidence the existing score needs to move

This additional control was specified before viewing the bounded credit predictions. Reuse their original roles, similarities, family exclusions, rating masks and limits. Compare the raw combined adjustment with a conservative version on every original panel.

For each contributing donor title use its personal rating utility minus its independent native forecast. On a chronological panel both that rating and the donor's training history must precede the prediction day. Across people retain the largest donor weight, then average errors within each independent donor family before aggregating families by their largest weight. A missing donor forecast provides no evidence. If the mean error and the proposed credit adjustment agree, apply the smaller absolute value with their shared sign. Otherwise keep the native score. Do not fit a coefficient or select thresholds on the forty dislikes.

Report changes to individual low and five-star ratings, crossings at 50 and 80, pair ordering and absolute error. A creator can help identify what someone likes even when the native score has already captured that preference.
