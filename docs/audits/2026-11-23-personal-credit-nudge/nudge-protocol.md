# Small personal credit adjustments

This offline protocol was fixed before viewing the new predictions. Test whether one shared creator earns a small adjustment, mixed personal ratings cancel and consistent ratings for similar work accumulate. Keep the frozen calibrated movie/show scorer and the exact original 32 panels. Exclude every target and its whole franchise, all co-held labels, masked ratings, and later or same-day ratings on chronological panels. Count verified direct personal star ratings only; no audience score, inferred opinion from a watch, or other user's rating enters the adjustment.

1. Treat a show's creator or showrunner and a film's director as lead credits. Separately recognise an exact Executive Producer role. Use stable person IDs. If one person has both roles on a work, the lead role takes precedence. Acting, writing, producer, associate producer and co-executive producer do not qualify.
2. A personal rating contributes a signed opinion `clamp((stars − 3) / 2, −1, 1)`. Four stars and two stars have equal and opposite strength. Three stars carry zero directional evidence. Compute a donor's descriptive similarity factor as one quarter plus three quarters times the Jaccard overlap of the target's and donor's genre sets. Multiply by 1 for lead/lead, ½ for executive/executive, and ¼ for cross-role evidence.
3. Each independent donor franchise counts once per person. Combine its ratings by weighted mean and take its strongest weight as family mass. For the person compute total mass M, signed vote S and absolute vote A across families. Their contribution is `cap × S / (3 + M) × abs(S) / A`, or zero with no absolute evidence. The cap is eight points for a target lead and four for a target executive. The three neutral family-equivalents keep one same-kind lead's five-star donor to two points. Persistent consistent preferences approach the cap; agreement on dissimilar titles grows more slowly.
4. Combine people by weighted mean. A family shared by several credited people gives them a combined blend weight of at most its strongest weight; distribute that blend weight proportionally, rather than adding their votes to the score. If both target leads and executives have evidence, executives receive at most one third of the final blend. A person with genuinely mixed ratings has evidence of zero or weak preference and remains in the average. Apply the single signed change to the native score, clamp and round once.

Prespecified arms are the native baseline, lead/lead only, executive/executive only (both works have an executive-only role for that person), and all qualifying roles. Report the incremental effect of adding executives to leads, including cross-role evidence. Pair order, absolute error, direction and mean size on dislikes, new red dislikes, liked titles turned red, individual five-star scores and green crossings all matter. Equally sized opposing opinions cancel exactly when genre/role weights are equal. Family-average/similarity and the strength of personal opinion determine cancellation otherwise.
