# Can local agreement earn a small audience adjustment?

Fix this specification before scoring. Use the same frozen native predictions, canonical franchise groups, private nested donor forecasts, rating masks and 32 panels as the credit replay. Use only personal ratings available for the fold. On date folds use only earlier rating days. TMDB's actual archived aggregate and vote count were collected later. These are retrospective inputs.

For each movie or show, compare up to twelve other independently rated franchises of the same medium. Rank potential comparisons by the mean of genre and tag Jaccard overlaps, break ties by stable work ID, and retain the most similar eligible work per franchise. Compare only positive overlaps. Restrict candidates to donors with an independent native forecast, a verified rating and a valid TMDB average and vote count. Select donors without consulting their stars, prediction errors or audience averages. Exclude the target's canonical franchise in every split. At least two donor franchises are needed.

Weight each donor by its metadata overlap times its vote reliability, votes / (votes + 50). Center reception against the weighted average of these comparable works. A gap of two points on TMDB's ten-point scale gives full directional strength; larger gaps stay capped. Multiply by the target's own vote reliability and a confidence factor of N / (N + 3), where N is the effective number of donor families. A maximum of four displayed-match points is available in either direction before these discounts.

1. **Small public nudge** uses the signed adjustment above with no personal agreement check.
2. **Personal agreement** multiplies that nudge by the positive part of the weighted Kendall rank correlation between the donor audience averages and this person's star ratings, allowing personal and crowd ties. A high crowd score earns more influence only when the viewer consistently prefers the higher-reception neighbours. A zero or negative relationship supplies no directional evidence. This makes no assumption that disliking popular titles implies liking unpopular ones.
3. **Personal agreement plus error check** also requires independent Coast prediction errors on neighbours on the target's side of the local audience average to point in the same direction as the nudge. Apply the smaller absolute correction, like the personal credit test. If either side has no data, keep the baseline.

Compare all three conditions on the same small budget. Check the earlier strict-local audience rule at the same maximum size separately. Report display-level ties, ordering and individual losses against the frozen baseline and, as a sensitivity, layer the exact same signed delta onto the recently tested guarded-lead display. Track the 40 dislikes, all 125 five-stars, the 93 who exceed the audience by at least one full star, and errors among four-star-or-better titles. Retain the original zero-rating abstentions. Classify individual named examples only after scoring. This experiment introduces no application or provider changes.
