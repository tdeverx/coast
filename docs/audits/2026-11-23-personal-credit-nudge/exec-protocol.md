# Does the executive producer's identity add information?

Use the signed creator/producer adjustment in nudge-protocol.md without fitting or tuning it. Compare its lead-only prediction with the full prediction on the original full-feedback work folds and verified earlier-day folds. Keep the film and series baselines, ratings, genre features, franchises, dates and lead credits fixed.

The primary control randomly reassigns whole exclusive executive-producer teams between titles with the same medium, broad genre and exact number of exclusive executive credits. A swap that would give a lead a second credit on the same title is ineligible. This retains team membership, title credit density, person credit frequencies, and genre strata. Freeze the broad strata in this order: Animation, Documentary, Reality, Comedy, Crime, Drama, Action & Adventure, Science Fiction, then the first remaining genre or unknown. Generate 99 reproducible alternatives without reading ratings. Each alternative uses the same descriptive credits across the folds and both arms always exclude the target franchise, co-held ratings and any ineligible donor.

The secondary control leaves every target's actual credits fixed and swaps only the eligible donors' executive teams inside each fold. This also preserves every producer's exact eligible training credit count. Evidence from a target executive who was a donor's lead stays genuine in this secondary control.

The primary comparison uses the full model's improvement in absolute error over the lead-only model on the original continuous score, paired by target. Positive improvement means predictions moved closer to personal ratings. Report the percentile/rank among shuffled teams (one-sided Monte Carlo tail with the observed assignment added) and secondary displayed changes including new red dislikes, green favourites, and the number of movies/shows and families carrying instrumental executive-only evidence. Also break out evidence for matches sharing an executive in the same role, and those transferring between executive and lead roles. The control tests identity beyond the retained genre strata and credit density; finer subgenre, franchises with different IDs, production partnerships and era may still explain associations. The whole and dated panels reuse many works and serve as separate checks for one viewer.
