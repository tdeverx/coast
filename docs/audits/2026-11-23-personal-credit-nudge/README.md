# Do shared creators improve personal match scores?

In this account, a small creator adjustment helped order favourites ahead of dislikes a little more often. Executive-producer credits connected more works, but using the actual producer identities did not reliably improve predictions. The experiment used the existing score as its starting point and never used the held title's personal rating to predict it.

## Comparing the same 40 dislikes and 125 five-star favourites

| Adjustment | Dislikes lower / same / higher | Five-stars lower / same / higher | Five-stars at 80 or above |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Original score | 0 / 40 / 0 | 0 / 125 / 0 | 111 |
| Creator or director | 0 / 38 / 2 | 0 / 83 / 42 | 111 |
| Creator or director, plus executive producers | 0 / 37 / 3 | 0 / 74 / 51 | 111 |
| Combined credits, with the error check below | 0 / 39 / 1 | 0 / 93 / 32 | 111 |
| Creator or director alone, with the error check | 0 / 39 / 1 | 0 / 101 / 24 | 111 |

The 40 dislikes all started above 50. Only two started low enough for even the maximum eight-point creator adjustment to take them below 50. Twenty-six had no rated comparison sharing an eligible credit. Four had negative adjustments smaller than half a displayed point. Another limitation was the underlying history. No credited person on any held title had consistently negative ratings on two or more eligible independent franchises. Equal but opposite evidence cancels in the formula. The real history tested repeated positive evidence much more extensively than repeated negative evidence.

## Comparing the order of titles

The error check asks whether the existing score also undershot or overshot the person's ratings on other works by this creator. If the direction agrees, it permits the smaller of the proposed credit adjustment and the average prediction error on those works. This helps avoid counting a known preference twice. The combined check was specified before seeing the new predictions. Running the same check with leads alone was an exploratory follow-up after seeing the producer control.

The figures below show how often a four-star-or-better title ranked above a title rated 2½ stars or less in the same fold, with half credit for ties.

| Holdout | Comparable pairs | Original | All credits | All credits with check | Leads with check |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Movies, whole title | 505 | 73.27% | 73.07% | 74.95% | 75.05% |
| Shows, whole title | 1,871 | 83.70% | 84.39% | 84.26% | 84.05% |
| Movies, earlier ratings only | 65 | 89.23% | 80.77% | 89.23% | 89.23% |
| Shows, earlier ratings only | 202 | 53.71% | 55.69% | 54.70% | 54.95% |

On the previously selected forty most-similar dislike–favourite pairs, all variants placed the five-star title higher in 34 pairs, tied twice and reversed four. The average lead for the favourite was 10.35 points originally, 10.85 with all credits and 10.58 for leads with the check. These pairs reuse 29 distinct favourites. Across all differently rated pairs in the chronological movie test, leads with the check slipped from 55.06% to 54.89%. On chronological shows they moved from 60.47% to 60.53%. The improvements are small and this is one viewer.

An individual high-scoring miss is compatible with a useful match score. Among the 134 held shows originally scoring 70–79, the viewer gave 111 at least 3½ stars, including 80 at four stars or higher. Thirteen received 2½ stars or less. This describes personally rated shows in this replay. It does not establish a probability of liking an unseen show.

## Reproducing the checks

The frozen replay contains 873 distinct personally rated titles, 32 panels and 13,477 scored appearances across alternative splits and rating budgets. Use the original inputs and a new owner-only destination for each individual ledger. The nested donor forecasts used for the error check are generated by the earlier [independent prediction procedure](../2026-11-23-personal-dislike-signals/lineage.md).

```sh
python3 -B residual.py CAPTURE CREDIT_METADATA VERIFIED_RATING_DATES NATIVE_GRADED INNER_FORECASTS PRIVATE_LEDGER
```

The evidence supports keeping the guarded lead signal as an offline candidate and leaving executive credits available for inspection. A second viewer or new rating period would be a worthwhile independent check before changing displayed scores.
