## Download-cap removal

The user subsequently requested removal of the download limit. Local admission, comic source download, native compressed-input admission and EPUB download/preparation no longer impose the former 128 MiB file cap. The authenticated reading proxy explicitly opts out of its former 512 MiB transfer cap (`maxBytes: Infinity`); shared provider defaults and other callers remain unchanged. This applies to every reader using these shared owners, including PDF proxy delivery.

Cancellation, source/version/permission checks, single-range validation, transfer idle deadlines, entry counts, expanded archive budgets, per-page output/pixel limits and decoder execution budgets remain in force. EPUB streaming now also races pending reads against cancellation and cancels/releases its body on exit. Empty native archives still fail explicitly. Removing the download cap permits larger retained compressed blobs; it is not a measured memory or speed improvement and does not guarantee arbitrary archives can decode within the independent extraction/browser budgets.

The opt-in actual-document regression is `COAST_READING_LARGE_MANIFEST=/private/path/manifest.json bun test tests/reading-comic-source.test.ts`. Supply an actual document above 512 MiB with private `file`, `bytes` and `sha256` fields. It checks local admission, complete replay with known and unknown lengths, progress byte totals and exact full-file hashes; normal runs skip this media-dependent case. Genuine Jellyfin acquisition and browser acceptance are recorded separately below.
