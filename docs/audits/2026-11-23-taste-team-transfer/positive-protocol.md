# Does enthusiasm transfer between the creative team's unrelated works?

This protocol was fixed before the positive forecasts were examined. The original private personal model, targets, rating masks and 32 panels are frozen. An enthusiastic prior opinion means at least 4½ stars. A target can gain only when every accessible opinion from the identical director set (film) or creator/showrunner set (show), outside its franchise, was enthusiastic. A mixed opinion vetoes transfer. Each contributing donor needs at least one half genre-set Jaccard overlap and an independently generated personal score. Include each donor franchise once. The forecast can rise by the positive part of the mean earlier personal error, using the existing personal star utility and display rounding.

Compare three positive alternatives: one donor family, two donor families, and one donor with an adjustment size learned on **other** lead teams. For the last alternative, generate training recipient errors from the existing family-excluded native predictions. Treat each training recipient as unknown, use the same transfer rule on the rest, then compare its proposed correction to its personal prediction error. On date panels only earlier rating days may supply donors. In estimating adjustment strength, hold out the outer target's entire creative team. Weight teams equally, require eight other supported teams and lower squared error than the original predictions for full-strength transfer on the training sample. Estimate a nonnegative least-squares multiplier of at most one. Unsupported adjustments retain the original score. Report the union of the full one-donor positive and previously specified negative signals as a separate symmetric comparison.

Assess recovery of five-star titles originally below green, newly green low (≤2½ stars), weak (≤3) and middling (3½) targets, and within-fold rating order. Report raw changes even when no threshold is crossed. Include distinct recipient/donor works, unordered transfer pairs and teams, and test how many recurrences survive excluding the largest contributing team. A truly unknown team with zero prior exact-team donors necessarily abstains. An extra date check admits only donor estimates themselves previously evaluated on the rolling rating-day panel, excluding reconstructed forecasts of the initial ratings batch. Every personal rating and allowed donor must predate its target at day resolution on date panels. The copy's catalogue credits are contemporary. Strict event-time replay and post-experiment prospective validation will be reported distinctly.

### Follow-up on incomplete franchise identities

After inspecting the baseline results, several nominally different franchises shared story worlds. As an exploratory conservative check, remove a donor if either title has an explicit spin-off keyword; cast IDs are missing on either side or overlap; both titles share a named universe keyword; or both have the same first meaningful title token. Retain the original all-opinions veto and rerun all masks. These exclusions can remove genuinely unrelated works. Titles passing them can still have undiscovered relationships.

### Follow-up on confirmation among already-green works

Does qualifying for a rise tell us anything more about actual enjoyment than the original high score already did? Pair each raised target with at most three titles whose score was unchanged in the same fold, whose original score lies within three points, and with at least one half genre-set Jaccard overlap. Exclude the target's own team and franchise, choose at most one title from each comparison franchise, and break ties by original score distance, genre overlap and canonical ID. Compare mean personal stars and the four-plus rate against the controls, once per matched target and once per creative team. This exploratory comparison reads held labels only after the comparison titles have been determined. Controls can be shared, so their count and team count are reported.
