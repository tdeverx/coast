# Returning individual leads and writers

The earlier result involved four shows and two identical creative teams. This exploratory extension fixes its credit and scoring rules before examining any predictions. Score each new route independently, then measure its incremental contribution to the original exact-team policy.

- A returning lead requires a credited director on both films or a credited creator/showrunner on both shows. The two nonempty lead sets must share a person and differ. A solo director working with different writers is already an exact lead match and supplies no new lead coverage here.
- A returning writer requires an explicit Writer, Screenplay, Teleplay or Head Writer credit on both works. The known lead teams must be disjoint, so this tests genuinely different creative leads. Empty lead teams cannot demonstrate a change. One arm accepts a recurring writer; the narrower comparison also requires at least half overlap between the two writing teams.
- For each shared person independently, take all directly rated training works in that same role outside the target franchise. Any rating above 2½ vetoes that person's penalty, regardless of genre or whether that work has an independent baseline score. For that person, only an eligible donor under the selected route can move the score. It needs the existing one-half genre overlap and its own independently withheld baseline. Group its prediction errors by franchise and give one vote per family. If several people provide qualifying evidence, pool each donor only once. Report both one and two independently disliked donor-family requirements.
- Retain the original score for absent evidence. Calculate the correction on the original 0–100 personal utility scale. Drop the frozen baseline by the mean negative donor prediction error, using a floor of zero. In the combined diagnostic the established exact-team rule takes precedence. New additions are computed only for titles the original rule did not change. Credit and genre completeness describe measured opportunities; missing metadata supplies no opinion.
- Use the same 32 panels, work/franchise masks, verified rating-day cutoffs and independent inner personal scores as the original study. Record newly red dislikes outside the four already seen, any penalties to favourites/liked/middling titles and any additional repeated teams. Track opportunities eliminated by the consensus veto and all-control data coverage. No other viewer's data, audience averages, vote counts or popularity enter this test.
