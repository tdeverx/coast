# Independent availability of television rating histories

The copied Coast server has one profile with enough directly rated movies or shows to repeat the full model. For a separate series-specific check, match official MovieLens releases to known Coast series by exact IMDb identifiers. Movie and series identifiers that conflict, ambiguous edition matches, and works missing a lead or genre are ineligible. Do not use a film's TMDB numeric identifier to match a series. An archive marketed for movies samples an unusual subset of series, often miniseries.

Before scoring, fix a per-viewer rule. Sort that viewer's opinions by UTC rating day. A title can use only its own viewer’s earlier opinions about another series with exactly the same nonempty creator/showrunner set; same-day ratings cannot predict each other. All prior opinions of the team must be at most 2½ stars for a negative signal or at least 4½ stars for a positive one. Each direction requires a similar-genre donor (Jaccard ≥½). Exclude matching recorded franchises/collections, identical normalised titles, and films/episodes. The stricter arm needs two distinct donor franchises. Report the distribution of actual target stars, unique teams/viewers, and both helpful and contradictory outcomes. This is a direct test of transfer between opinions, independent of any invented external Coast card percentage.

Of the 17 unique verified series in MovieLens latest-small, four had both genres and complete-enough creator information in the Coast capture. In MovieLens 25M the corresponding counts were 62 and 27. In both archives there were zero pairs with matching lead creators, suitable genre overlap and different recorded families. This is a coverage limit. Neither release can confirm the four personal television predictions on other TV viewers.
