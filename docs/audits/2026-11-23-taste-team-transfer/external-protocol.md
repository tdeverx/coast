# External movie-viewer check

Test the already specified rule in the current small GroupLens sample, separately for each anonymous viewer. Use official MovieLens-to-IMDb/TMDB IDs, all IMDb director IDs and Coast's saved descriptive movie metadata where confidently matched. Archive only aggregate results. Do not use MovieLens averages, TMDB scores, popularity, other users' ratings or approximate title matches.

For each viewer hold the final approximately 20% of their movie ratings by original date, keeping every UTC rating day intact. Require at least 25 strictly earlier ratings. Discard viewers whose initial import day contains the proposed holdout boundary if separating the day leaves fewer than 25 earlier ratings. All earlier personal ratings of a known exact director team can veto a penalty at three stars or above. Compare only targets and donors with matched Coast metadata (genres, film director credits and franchise identity); do not allow a transfer between instalments of the same franchise. Verify that IMDb's full set and Coast's cast-credit director set have a one-to-one correspondence for paired films; disagreements defer. A donor and target must have genre Jaccard similarity at least one half. One control requires any prior disliked family; another requires two. Unknown franchises on unmapped prior works only broaden the protective veto.

At each user's cutoff fit the same calibrated movie model as the private Coast replay, with that viewer's earlier direct ratings only. Missing Coast metadata on other training works may use MovieLens genre, IMDb director and release decade. Do not use another viewer's labels. For donor residuals, hold the donor family out of the personal fit using three fixed inner franchise folds. Report as an external retrospective test on movies, separate from the owner's movie/show holdouts. Credit metadata is current.

Report the number of actual dislikes corrected below 50, liked titles incorrectly turned red, individual five-star predictions lowered or leaving green, coverage, participating users and independent teams. Also report the raw personal ratings of all changed targets. Retain favourable and unfavourable outcomes.

The small fixture provided only seven transfer opportunities. Before scoring the larger sample, fix the same procedure on the stable MovieLens 25M benchmark, selecting users by `SHA256("coast-team-external-25m:" + userId)[0] mod 16 == 0`. Keep those results separate because the two GroupLens releases can share viewers and titles. Drop any nonunique movie identity. Two films must have identical full director sets within both the IMDb and Coast ID namespaces. Number of directors must match across namespaces for each film.

After observing the first 25M group's errors, fix an independent sensitivity check on disjoint viewers from the same release with hash remainder **one**. Test the identical full negative transfer and an attenuated version that multiplies the downward prediction error by **k / (k + 1)** before rounding, where k is the number of independent previously disliked families. One donor supplies half a shift and two supply two thirds. Change no other settings. Report the fixed one- and two-donor minima, red outcomes, five-star loss, improvements versus deteriorations and paired mean absolute personal-utility error. A weaker score adjustment requires improvements on this untouched viewer sample to justify even further consideration.
