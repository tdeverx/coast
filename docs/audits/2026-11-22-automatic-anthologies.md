# Automatic anthology seasons and provider identity

Coast can display independently numbered miniseries as seasons of one show when their TMDB shows share a TVDB series ID, have distinct release dates, and provide distinct, complete episode IDs. The group follows original premiere order. The existing season and episode tables hold Coast’s hierarchy, and the existing provider identity table holds each season’s source show. The app does not contact TVDB.

Legacy collisions are evaluated before the catalogue changes. The transaction preserves the UUID and watch events of each episode with a verified owner. Retained Trakt history records prove ownership on a collapsed episode. If past watches or attached files cannot identify one owner, the catalogue stays as it was and the conflict is logged. On live Coast a read-only census found 19 separate TMDB episode IDs attached to 10 Coast episodes and verified all ten of the recorded watches against Hill House.

Trakt season ratings use the corresponding standalone show. Ratings on the grouped Coast show stay local, including during absence reconciliation. Episode history follows its individual provider identity. Seerr delivery and Jellyfin ingestion translate between each story’s native season and Coast’s season number. A grouped request targets the selected story.

## Work bounds and verification

- Discovery checks only already-linked TMDB identities for the same Coast show. Exceptional grouping reads are bounded to twelve stories, with at most three provider requests in flight. It verifies at most 128 episodes before writes. The confirmed path uses the existing indexed identity lookups, and the warm Trakt episode fixture verified zero TMDB requests.
- Provider data arrives before the transaction. A single advisory-lock batch fences alias ownership and a show lock serializes changes to its hierarchy. The transaction keeps the original tracking events and publishes the season positions and alias changes together.
- The PostgreSQL fixtures cover reverse discovery order, a later third story, independent season ratings, retention of an older Coast rating, Trakt round-tripping, a verified collapsed episode and an ambiguous manual watch. The latter fixture leaves its history and aliases untouched.
- A separate rehearsal copied the live anthology’s local and provider episode IDs, season alias, and ten verified watch records into disposable PostgreSQL. Controlled TMDB responses provided the two story catalogues. The repair produced ten episodes in season one and nine in season two. Every copied watch row and every season-one episode UUID matched before and after. This scoped rehearsal made zero production writes.
- An existing Coast import failed with SQLSTATE `21000` when two recommendations resolved to one record. The shared TMDB importer retains the first position per Coast ID in one pass over at most 20 recommendations. Trakt skips optional recommendations and the detail view loads them on demand. Deterministic duplicate-write errors receive their own diagnostic instead of retrying as a connection outage.
- Playback fades the controller and post-play panel directly. No listeners or timers were added. On a controlled 1024×576 video, three picture samples stayed at RGB 144 through idle and pointer reveal in Chromium and Firefox. Visible shading remained within the header and controller. The reported steady full-frame dim was absent in the original Chromium fixture, so the device-specific report remains unverified.
