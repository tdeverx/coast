# Deliberate drops in title taste

The existing full-history aggregation distinguishes a direct drop from a dropped episode whose features roll up to a show. A direct drop supplies the signed affinity and weight of a one-star opinion. A below-neutral rating on the same work increases its weight up to twice the base weight. The work still contributes one observation per affinity and keeps its actual rating for display. A positive rating retains its explicit opinion, and a dropped title no longer receives a return boost. The rating-only adjustment continues to train on recorded stars. An experiment that also fed dropped works into that separate centered model could weaken its negative adjustment as the training mean changed.

The refresh uses the existing history query, adding a single grouped Boolean. It adds no request, provider call, or new table to profile refresh or candidate scoring. The existing revision forces a rebuild and fresh recommendation scores. The PostgreSQL fixtures distinguish a child drop from a direct drop, and the scoring fixtures compare one star, a direct drop, and a direct drop with a low rating on the same feature cohort. The inspected live account had no dropped screen works, so the strength comparison is fixture evidence.
