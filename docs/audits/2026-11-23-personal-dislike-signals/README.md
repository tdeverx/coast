# Can this viewer's other titles distinguish their dislikes from similar favourites?

The strongest signal was a previous low rating for a similar work by the identical lead creative team. On the original 873 whole-title predictions it put four of 40 actual dislikes below 50 while preserving the scores of all 613 four-star-or-better titles and every middling title. All four were shows. This success comes from two pairs of shows, each predicting the other. Only one case repeated using strictly earlier rating dates. The experiment supplies a candidate for further personal validation. It does not establish a reliable production rule.

A [follow-up on creative teams](../2026-11-23-taste-team-transfer/README.md) measures other shows, films, positive predictions and independent movie viewers, and freezes a forward comparison for this viewer.

The studio and behavioural controls found that repeated exposure or moving on to other media could be ambiguous. One show classified as an observable early exit received five stars. Simply completing a film could not separate any of the seven disliked movies from the five-star films. In the fixed set of paired works, 28 of the 40 dislikes themselves had traits backed by multiple highly rated works. Only seven showed a repeated exclusive negative trait in those comparisons. The identical-team follow-up excludes the target and its entire franchise, keeps each rating mask, counts one vote per independent donor franchise and vetoes a penalty if any allowed prior opinion of the team exceeds 2½ stars. Its narrower version compares genre sets using the half-overlap rule previously used in the studio experiment. We tried this after seeing a director make both a disliked musical and a liked period drama. Its four whole-title successes and the removal of that specific error are exploratory. [Authorship model](lineage.md) · [Identical lead teams](lineage-exact.md) · [Same team and similar genre](same-kind.md)

“Earlier” refers to the original, verified personal rating date. A whole-title holdout can use the viewer's rating of a different title recorded later. The date test can use only ratings from preceding days. Ratings from the target day stay together, including bulk imports. Date order limits which personal opinions a prediction may see; it gives older opinions no scoring bonus. Current catalogue descriptions are used to identify credits and genres. No TMDB audience score, vote count, popularity signal or other viewer’s opinion enters these experiments. Both serving scorers retain their existing policies.
