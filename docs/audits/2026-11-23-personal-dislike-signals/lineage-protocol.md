# Personal prediction errors shared by creative teams

This offline test uses the same frozen 32 panels and the copy's verified direct star ratings. Its rules were fixed before checking its predictions. One other show from the same two creators can be more relevant than one show sharing a single writer. Test whether Coast's **errors** repeat across independent works with a shared creative team. A low personal rating already predicted by the baseline supplies little correction.

1. Use only stable IDs of credited people. Film leads are directors. Show leads are creators and showrunners. Include writers, screenplay, teleplay and head-writer credits in the writing team. Exclude the performers, generic producers, character creators credited only for underlying intellectual property, franchise identity and audience reception. Deduplicate people across roles. Current descriptive credits are a retrospective snapshot.
2. For each possible donor compare the target's and donor's sets of leads and of all authors. If both share a lead, similarity is the mean of their two Jaccard overlaps. Otherwise use half their authorship Jaccard overlap. Keep positive similarities, square them, and give every outside franchise at most one vote, from its most similar work or the average across tied works. Exclude all ratings in the held-out fold and exclude the target's entire franchise even in whole-title tests.
3. The proposed change is the weighted mean of other titles' errors on the existing star utility scale, attenuated by the total similarity weight up to one. Every donor error uses a native personal prediction made with its franchise held out, or an earlier date prediction inside the dated panels. The independent native predictions were saved during a prior evaluation and contain only title IDs and Coast's personal scores. All audience data is excluded.
4. Learn whether to trust a proposal inside the permitted training fold by predicting every training title's residual from other families. Keep four evidence groups, splitting positive and negative proposals separately for shared multiple authors with at least one shared lead, and other authorship overlap. Each validated recipient franchise counts once per group. Require eight independent families and lower squared error than leaving scores unchanged. Estimate the multiplier by least squares, shrink with four conservative family-equivalent observations and cap it at one. On dates, use only donors from strictly earlier rating days when calibrating. Use the same masks as the native scorer. Missing scores and unsupported groups retain the personal baseline.
5. Report coverage, captured dislikes under 50, penalties to liked and five-star targets, ordering and named inspection examples privately. Include the unconstrained proposed change to distinguish insufficient evidence from conservative admission. No new production score is selected on these tests.
