# Sensitivity test requiring local audience agreement

This follow-up was specified after measuring the medium/conditional residual corrections. Its settings were fixed before looking at its own held-out scores. Run on the same 32 panels with the same baseline, inner native score residuals, genre/tag features, vote reliability, minimum support and one-standard-error positive slope.

A title can now move only on evidence from a matching individual genre or tag. Zero eligible traits means zero change. The correction comes from that trait's slope alone; a medium slope cannot qualify it or contribute any points. Check low and high audience reception separately, relative to the vote-weighted mean of the trait. Require the direction also to agree with the title's side of the medium mean. The medium mean determines direction only.

Within each permitted outer training set, generate a predicted correction using an entire held-out franchise partition at a time. In each trait-by-side cell, give each distinct franchise one vote. Admit the corresponding low/high direction only when at least eight independently scored families are present, a strict majority of families have personal residuals in the same direction, and cross-fitted squared error improves over the unchanged private scorer. Require nonzero corrections for the cross-fit check. Within an admitted cell, cap the correction at the scaling supported by held examples. Average multiple eligible terms within a feature family and then across genre and tag, so the same audience score cannot be applied repeatedly. Report favourite score drops and green losses separately.
