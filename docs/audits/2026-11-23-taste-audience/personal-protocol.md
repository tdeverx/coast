# Personal TMDB agreement experiment

Use the sealed, calibrated movie/show score as the comparative baseline. Keep the exact original whole-title, two franchise, rolling-day, zero-rating and sparse-rating cohorts. An unknown audience mean/count or fewer than 16 previous direct ratings with usable scores leaves the personal baseline in place. A five-star work whose personal rating exceeds the crowd by at least one star is a disagreement favourite (personal stars minus raw TMDB / 2). Report every downgrade among them, plus lost and gained green favourites separately.

Estimate the correction to Coast's personal score from its prediction error on independent ratings: map personal stars to the same published utility scale (3 is 50, 4 is 80, 5 is 100). Each training title's score must have been predicted with its own franchise fold or rating day excluded. The outer target and all other works withheld by the original split stay out of each training step. Learn the slope from *within-training* changes in TMDB score and personal residual, with a separate intercept to avoid learning a global rating bump. Apply only the slope: an average reception cannot change the baseline. Model the global medium and individual genres/tags. For each trait use matched observations, shrink toward no correction, and combine overlapping supported traits as a weighted mean rather than adding the same audience vote several times. If an audience score predicts v2's errors poorly or with the opposite sign in the viewer's local data, reduce or withhold its influence.

Use vote reliability n/(n+50) in both training and scoring; 50 votes give half strength. Fit a slope only with at least 16 independent work ratings, reduce the estimated positive slope by one standard error and use the ratio of residual variance explained when applied on held-out families to decide eligibility. Local trait evidence uses at least eight work ratings and shrinks toward the medium result with 12 observations of prior strength. For comparison, measure the medium's slope without local modifiers, then the full contextual estimate; retain the ungated contextual estimate as a diagnostic. Raw scoring and the private title labels stay separate. Assess against recovery of dislikes below 50, any penalty to disagreement favourites, paired green losses and liked false reds; average error or better ordering alone is insufficient.

Current TMDB aggregates are retrospective covariates. A rating date controls access to the owner's history, not the vintage of a provider aggregate. Historical availability and inferred accuracy must be reported separately.
